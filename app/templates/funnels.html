What a funnel here can and cannot tell you

Steps are counted within one visit. A visitor ID is derived from a salt that rotates at this site's midnight, so somebody who reads the pricing page today and signs up on Thursday is two unrelated people as far as this database is concerned. That is the same rotation that makes the numbers here anonymous; it is not a setting.

Funnels read raw events rather than the daily aggregates, because a funnel is a question about the order of several things and the aggregates hold one at a time. {% if retention_days %} Raw events are kept for {{ retention_days }} days here, so a funnel cannot look further back than that. {% else %} Raw events are kept indefinitely on this instance, so funnels can look as far back as the data goes. {% endif %}
